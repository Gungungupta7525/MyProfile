01 — About

A bit of context

I work on two things that happen to overlap more than people expect: enterprise automation and applied machine learning.

At HCLTech I build automations on SAP BTP — extracting invoice data, writing it out, triggering notifications, and driving the SAP GUI itself where an API doesn't reach. Most of that work is less about the interesting technology and more about being the person who actually talks to the business team and works out which manual step they're actually complaining about.

The AI side is where I push myself hardest. My main project, ScraperAgent, turns a job description into a ranked list of real candidate profiles by searching, deduplicating, and scoring results across multiple queries. I built it because I wanted to solve something end to end — search, ranking, caching, a backend, a frontend, and deployment — rather than a notebook that runs once.

What I'm looking for next is a team where I can keep doing both things, and go deeper on whichever one the business needs most.
